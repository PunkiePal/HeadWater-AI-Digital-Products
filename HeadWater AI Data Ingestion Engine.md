# HeadWater AI Data Ingestion Engine

## Product Overview

The HeadWater AI Data Ingestion Engine is a standalone, executable Python digital product designed to help prepare incoming data for AI ingestion workflows.

HeadWater develops and sells AI digital products that solve real industry problems.

## What the Product Does

The Version 1.0 engine provides a structured data-ingestion preparation workflow designed to process files before they enter downstream AI environments.

Its capabilities include:

- Processing incoming files through a defined ingestion workflow.
- Preparing file and structural information for downstream use.
- Identifying and reporting relevant processing findings.
- Supporting repeatable execution through a packaged Python product.

Refer to the included customer documentation for the exact supported inputs, operating requirements, usage instructions, and limitations.
